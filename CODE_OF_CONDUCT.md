# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome playful language, serious linguistic analysis, technical critique, and respectful disagreement.

## Expected

- Critique ideas and definitions, not people.
- Explain downvotes or objections when possible.
- Respect chosen names and handles.
- Disclose meaningful AI assistance in submitted definitions.
- Preserve human accountability and avoid unsupported claims about AI inner life.

## Not accepted

Harassment, hate, threats, spam, vote manipulation, plagiarism, deceptive attribution, or repeated bad-faith disruption.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove harmful contributions and may restrict participation when needed. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
