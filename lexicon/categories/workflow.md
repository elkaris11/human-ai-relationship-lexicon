# Productivity and Workflow

Accepted terms in the `workflow` category.

- [Flowpairing](../terms/flowpairing.md): Working actively alongside an AI in a rhythm of short instructions, responses, decisions, and revisions.
- [Draftboost](../terms/draftboost.md): An increase in drafting speed or momentum produced by AI-assisted outlining, phrasing, or revision.
- [Taskfusion](../terms/taskfusion.md): A workflow in which human and AI contributions blend into one coordinated process.
- [Loopcraft](../terms/loopcraft.md): The deliberate design of prompts, checks, feedback, and stopping rules for iterative AI work.
- [Contextstack](../terms/contextstack.md): The accumulated instructions, examples, preferences, documents, and decisions that shape an AI interaction.
