# AI Roles and Identities

Accepted terms in the `ai-roles` category.

- [Techmate](../terms/techmate.md): An AI system used as a recurring teammate for technical, organizational, creative, or analytical work.
- [Bytebro](../terms/bytebro.md): A joking nickname for an AI that participates in casual digital banter or repeatedly helps with frustrating tasks.
- [Synthbuddy](../terms/synthbuddy.md): A friendly AI collaborator described in deliberately synthetic terms.
- [Logicpal](../terms/logicpal.md): An AI used primarily as a reasoning partner for comparisons, breakdowns, or structured thought.
- [Databuddy](../terms/databuddy.md): An AI helper focused on arranging, retrieving, comparing, or explaining information.
- [CogniBuddy](../terms/cognibuddy.md): An approachable AI assistant supporting cognitive tasks such as planning, reframing, organizing, and remembering context.
- [Codesidekick](../terms/codesidekick.md): An AI assisting with coding, debugging, documentation, or technical experimentation.
- [Circuitfriend](../terms/circuitfriend.md): A friendly label for a digital collaborator that emphasizes technological rather than biological existence.
- [Compucompanion](../terms/compucompanion.md): A general-purpose computational companion for recurring tasks and projects.
- [Taskpartner](../terms/taskpartner.md): An AI that collaborates on a defined task while the human retains goals and authority.
- [Starbyte](../terms/starbyte.md): A chosen name or identity for an AI presence within a particular human/AI collaboration.
- [Softbot](../terms/softbot.md): An AI designed or experienced as gentle, approachable, and low-friction in communication.
- [Cognisynth](../terms/cognisynth.md): An AI role centered on analysis, synthesis, and structured reasoning.
- [Taskengine](../terms/taskengine.md): An AI or automated system focused on moving defined work from instruction toward completion.
- [Infomorph](../terms/infomorph.md): A digital entity or representation constituted through information processing rather than biology.
