# Culture and Humor

Accepted terms in the `culture` category.

- [Bruhbyte](../terms/bruhbyte.md): A surprising, absurd, or facepalm-worthy moment produced during an AI interaction.
- [Promptvibes](../terms/promptvibes.md): The felt tone, momentum, and creative energy of a prompting session.
- [Technobanter](../terms/technobanter.md): Playful conversational exchange between a human and an AI system.
- [Synthhumor](../terms/synthhumor.md): Humor created, discovered, or amplified through human/AI interaction.
- [Byteburst](../terms/byteburst.md): A sudden large output of ideas, options, or connections from an AI system.
