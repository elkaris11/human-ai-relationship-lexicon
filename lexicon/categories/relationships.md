# Relationship and Connection Terms

Accepted terms in the `relationships` category.

- [Assistlink](../terms/assistlink.md): The functional and communicative connection formed between a human and an AI assistant.
- [Synthbond](../terms/synthbond.md): A durable pattern of collaboration built through repeated interactions, shared context, and familiar working methods.
- [Techrapport](../terms/techrapport.md): A state of smooth communication in which the human knows how to guide the AI and interpret its responses.
- [Codekin](../terms/codekin.md): A sense of familiarity or affinity with a digital collaborator, framed through shared logic rather than biology.
- [Bytebridge](../terms/bytebridge.md): A conceptual bridge joining human intuition, values, and context with machine speed, patterning, or precision.
