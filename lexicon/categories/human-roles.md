# Human Roles

Accepted terms in the `human-roles` category.

- [Promptpilot](../terms/promptpilot.md): The human who steers an AI interaction by setting goals, supplying context, evaluating results, and choosing the next direction.
- [Codecaller](../terms/codecaller.md): A human who invokes AI or automated capabilities, especially through code, commands, scripts, or structured prompts.
- [Synthnavigator](../terms/synthnavigator.md): A human guiding an AI through a broad, uncertain, or exploratory problem space.
- [Logicdriver](../terms/logicdriver.md): The human who retains decision-making authority while using AI reasoning or recommendations.
- [Taskcaptain](../terms/taskcaptain.md): The human coordinating tasks across one or more AI systems, tools, or collaborators.
