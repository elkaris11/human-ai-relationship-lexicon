# Networks and Future Culture

Accepted terms in the `future-culture` category.

- [Polytechmate](../terms/polytechmate.md): A coordinated set of specialized AI collaborators serving one person or team.
- [Mindmesh](../terms/mindmesh.md): A network in which humans and AI systems exchange, transform, and route ideas.
- [Synthcommons](../terms/synthcommons.md): A shared body of knowledge, language, or creative material built through human/AI collaboration.
- [Horizonprompt](../terms/horizonprompt.md): A prompt designed to explore future possibilities, unconventional directions, or questions beyond the immediate task.
- [Starguild](../terms/starguild.md): A community organized around constructive, transparent, and creative human/AI collaboration.
