# Interaction Modes

Accepted terms in the `interaction` category.

- [Promptplay](../terms/promptplay.md): Playful experimentation with wording, roles, examples, constraints, and formats to discover useful outputs.
- [Thinksync](../terms/thinksync.md): A moment or state in which a human’s developing thought and an AI’s response align in a particularly useful way.
- [Logicloop](../terms/logicloop.md): An iterative reasoning cycle in which the human questions, the AI responds, and each round refines the next.
- [Taskflow](../terms/taskflow.md): The practical sequence by which work moves between a human and an AI system.
- [Synthspark](../terms/synthspark.md): A new idea, connection, or direction ignited during interaction with an AI.
