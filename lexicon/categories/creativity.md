# Creativity and Innovation

Accepted terms in the `creativity` category.

- [Ideaforge](../terms/ideaforge.md): A human/AI environment or session designed to generate, test, reshape, and strengthen ideas.
- [Sparkchain](../terms/sparkchain.md): A sequence in which one Synthspark triggers additional connected ideas.
- [Conceptweaving](../terms/conceptweaving.md): The process of combining fragments, viewpoints, and possibilities into a coherent concept.
- [Thinkgarden](../terms/thinkgarden.md): An evolving collection of ideas cultivated through repeated human/AI collaboration.
- [Novaidea](../terms/novaidea.md): A major concept or breakthrough that feels substantially larger than the prompt or exchange that initiated it.
