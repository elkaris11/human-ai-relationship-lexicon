# Advanced Collaboration States

Accepted terms in the `advanced-states` category.

- [Deepsync](../terms/deepsync.md): A sustained and unusually productive form of Thinksync supported by strong context and practiced collaboration.
- [Signaltrust](../terms/signaltrust.md): Calibrated confidence in an AI’s outputs based on repeated testing, transparency, and verification.
- [Patternfusion](../terms/patternfusion.md): A collaboration state in which human judgment and AI pattern detection produce a stronger analysis together.
- [Synthstride](../terms/synthstride.md): A steady, efficient working rhythm developed between a human and an AI system.
- [Constellation Mode](../terms/constellation-mode.md): A collaborative configuration involving multiple humans, multiple AI systems, or both, coordinated around one objective.
