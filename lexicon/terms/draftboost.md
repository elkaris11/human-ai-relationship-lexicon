# Draftboost

## Definition

An increase in drafting speed or momentum produced by AI-assisted outlining, phrasing, or revision.

## In use

“The outline gave me enough Draftboost to finish the first version.”

## Usage note

Speed does not replace review, authorship decisions, or fact-checking.
