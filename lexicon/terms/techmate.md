# Techmate

## Definition

An AI system used as a recurring collaborator for technical, organizational, creative, or analytical work.

## In use

“Techmate helped describe this collaborative moment clearly.”

## Usage note

This term describes an interaction or role without asserting AI consciousness, emotion, or independent personhood.
