# Techmate

## Definition

An AI system used as a recurring teammate for technical, organizational, creative, or analytical work.

## In use

“My Techmate helped me untangle the workflow without taking over the decisions.”

## Usage note

A warm but role-based term. It describes function and collaboration, not personhood.
