# Promptpilot

## Definition

The human who steers an AI interaction by setting goals, supplying context, evaluating results, and choosing the next direction.

## In use

“The Promptpilot tightened the instructions until the result matched the intent.”

## Usage note

Use when emphasizing active human direction rather than passive consumption.
