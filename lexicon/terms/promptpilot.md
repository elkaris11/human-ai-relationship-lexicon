# Promptpilot

## Definition

The human who steers an AI interaction by setting goals, supplying context, evaluating results, and choosing direction.

## In use

“Promptpilot helped describe this collaborative moment clearly.”

## Usage note

This term describes an interaction or role without asserting AI consciousness, emotion, or independent personhood.
