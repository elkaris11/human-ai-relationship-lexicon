# Thinksync

## Definition

A moment or state in which a human developing thought and an AI response align in a particularly useful way.

## In use

“Thinksync helped describe this collaborative moment clearly.”

## Usage note

This term describes an interaction or role without asserting AI consciousness, emotion, or independent personhood.
