# Thinksync

## Definition

A moment or state in which a human’s developing thought and an AI’s response align in a particularly useful way.

## In use

“We hit Thinksync when it named the pattern I could feel but had not articulated.”

## Usage note

Describes perceived alignment in the interaction, not mind reading or shared consciousness.
