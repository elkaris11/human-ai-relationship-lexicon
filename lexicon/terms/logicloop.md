# Logicloop

## Definition

An iterative reasoning cycle in which the human questions, the AI responds, and each round refines the next.

## In use

“Three Logicloops exposed the hidden assumption in the plan.”

## Usage note

A healthy Logicloop includes verification and permission to reject the output.
