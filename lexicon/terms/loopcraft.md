# Loopcraft

## Definition

The deliberate design of prompts, checks, feedback, and stopping rules for iterative AI work.

## In use

“Good Loopcraft prevented endless rewriting without a decision.”

## Usage note

A useful discipline for repeatability, safety, and quality control.
