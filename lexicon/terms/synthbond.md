# Synthbond

## Definition

A durable pattern of collaboration built through repeated interactions, shared context, and familiar working methods.

## In use

“Months of iteration created a strong Synthbond around the project.”

## Usage note

Bond refers to continuity and usefulness, not proven emotional attachment by the AI.
