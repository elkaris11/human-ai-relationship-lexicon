# Horizonprompt

## Definition

A prompt designed to explore future possibilities, unconventional directions, or questions beyond the immediate task.

## In use

“The Horizonprompt asked what the system might enable five design generations later.”

## Usage note

Exploratory answers should be labeled as speculative.
