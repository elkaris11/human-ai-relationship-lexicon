# Logicpal

## Definition

An AI used primarily as a reasoning partner for comparisons, breakdowns, or structured thought.

## In use

“My Logicpal helped separate the assumptions from the evidence.”

## Usage note

Use for reasoning support, not as a claim that the system independently understands like a human.
