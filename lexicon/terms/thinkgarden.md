# Thinkgarden

## Definition

An evolving collection of ideas cultivated through repeated human/AI collaboration.

## In use

“My project notebook has become a Thinkgarden rather than a graveyard of notes.”

## Usage note

The gardening metaphor emphasizes care, pruning, and growth.
