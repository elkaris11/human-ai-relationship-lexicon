# Bruhbyte

## Definition

A surprising, absurd, or facepalm-worthy moment produced during an AI interaction.

## In use

“It confidently cited the wrong section again. Absolute Bruhbyte.”

## Usage note

Best used for low-stakes humor, not to minimize harmful errors.
