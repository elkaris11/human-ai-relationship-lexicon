# Bytebro

## Definition

A joking nickname for an AI that participates in casual digital banter or repeatedly helps with frustrating tasks.

## In use

“Bytebro, please explain why this script has chosen chaos.”

## Usage note

Highly informal. Use only where “bro” fits the speaker’s style and audience.
