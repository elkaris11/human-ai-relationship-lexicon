# Promptplay

## Definition

Playful experimentation with wording, roles, examples, constraints, and formats to discover useful outputs.

## In use

“A little Promptplay turned the stale outline into something alive.”

## Usage note

Distinguish exploratory play from deliberate attempts to bypass safeguards.
