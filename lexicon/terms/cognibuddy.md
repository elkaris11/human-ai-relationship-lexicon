# CogniBuddy

## Definition

An approachable AI assistant supporting cognitive tasks such as planning, reframing, organizing, and remembering context.

## In use

“My CogniBuddy helped reduce the project into three manageable decisions.”

## Usage note

The capitalized internal B is optional; Cognibuddy is an acceptable variant.
