# Codecaller

## Definition

A human who invokes AI or automated capabilities, especially through code, commands, scripts, or structured prompts.

## In use

“The Codecaller chained several small tools into one repeatable workflow.”

## Usage note

Useful in technical settings; it does not imply that the person wrote every underlying system.
