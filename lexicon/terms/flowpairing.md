# Flowpairing

## Definition

Working actively alongside an AI in a rhythm of short instructions, responses, decisions, and revisions.

## In use

“Flowpairing kept me engaged instead of dumping the whole project on the model.”

## Usage note

Modeled loosely on pair work, while preserving the distinction between human and system.
