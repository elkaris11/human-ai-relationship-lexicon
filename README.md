# Human/AI Relationship Lexicon 🌱

An open, community-governed vocabulary for human/AI collaboration without unsupported claims of AI personhood, consciousness, emotion, or reciprocity.

## The lifecycle

1. **Seed submitted** through the New Term issue form.
2. **Automated analysis** checks completeness, spelling overlap, definition overlap, category fit, and semantic neighbors.
3. **Thinkgarden** incubation gives the community time to test and discuss the term.
4. **Community vote** uses 👍 for support and 👎 for opposition on the proposal issue.
5. **Harvest review** evaluates vote totals, substantive feedback, clarity, distinctiveness, and real usage.
6. **Core Lexicon** receives accepted terms. Rejected and superseded proposals remain in the archive.

> The analyzer advises. It never decides. Similar meanings may become **Semantic Neighbors**, not automatic duplicates.

## Repository map

- `lexicon/` accepted terms and taxonomy
- `thinkgarden/` seedlings and incubation records
- `archive/` rejected, withdrawn, deprecated, and superseded proposals
- `automation/` validation, similarity analysis, vote tallying, and promotion helpers
- `.github/` issue forms, workflows, templates, and labels
- `docs/` governance, voting, style, moderation, and system design
