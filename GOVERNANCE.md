# Governance

## Roles

- **Promptpilots:** community contributors
- **Gardeners:** triage proposals and support incubation
- **Lexicographers:** maintain definitions and taxonomy
- **Maintainers:** administer the repository and releases

## Decision standard

Votes are important evidence, not an automatic popularity switch. Harvest review also considers clarity, distinctiveness, usefulness, usage evidence, safety, accessibility, and manipulation concerns.

## Conflict of interest

A maintainer who proposed a term may discuss it but should not be the sole person approving its harvest.

## Appeals

A rejected term may be resubmitted after material revision or new usage evidence. Link the earlier proposal and explain what changed.
