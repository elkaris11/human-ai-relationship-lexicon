# Terminology Relationships

This is the navigational backbone of the **Semantic Neighbors** system. Similarity does not automatically mean duplication. Related terms may differ by scope, register, role, intensity, or context.

## Relationship labels

- **Synonym:** substantially the same meaning
- **Near-synonym:** strong overlap with a meaningful distinction
- **Broader / narrower:** different scope
- **Register variant:** professional, playful, poetic, technical, or community-specific alternative
- **Contrast:** useful opposite or boundary term
- **Process neighbor:** adjacent step in a workflow or lifecycle
