# Founding Vision

Human language has words for coworkers, teammates, tools, muses, communities, mentors, and creative partners. As AI-assisted work became ordinary, a gap emerged: people needed language for interactive collaboration that was warmer and more precise than “just a tool,” without pretending an AI system is human.

The Human/AI Relationship Lexicon exists to cultivate that language in public.

## Our purpose

- Name recurring human/AI roles, workflows, creative moments, and cultural patterns.
- Preserve human agency, judgment, accountability, and authorship.
- Allow playful relational language without unsupported claims about AI consciousness, emotion, or reciprocity.
- Treat vocabulary as living community infrastructure rather than fixed doctrine.
- Welcome synonyms, register variants, forks, and documented disagreement.

## The Thinkgarden

New words begin as seedlings. They are discussed, tested, revised, and voted on in a greenhouse space before facing the big mean world of heavy usage. Mature terms may be harvested into the Core Lexicon. Unsuccessful seedlings are composted into an archive rather than erased, because rejected language can still teach future contributors.

## Invitation

Use the words that help. Revise the ones that do not. Plant what is still missing.
